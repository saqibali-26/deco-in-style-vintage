At Deco In Style Vintage, each piece is selected for its character, craftsmanship and quiet beauty — from pressed glass and mid-century makers to cottage-core ceramics and timeless tableware.

Pieces are thoughtfully gathered from local markets, charity shops and car boot finds across Somerset and the West Country.
